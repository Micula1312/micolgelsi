01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

Hyperarchiviation / studies.

RESEARCH OBJECT: hyperarchiviation as a situated artistic practice. Investigating how autonomous physical-digital archives can sustain spaces of resistance, self-education, shared memory and collective inquiry beyond dominant institutional and platform infrastructures.

CASE STUDIES: Archive of the Untamed / struggles for urban commons, spontaneous ecologies and collective memory; ALMA Futura / feminist self-education, research into bodies and technologies, and autonomous knowledge infrastructures. Two situated practices of resistance informing an evolving methodology.

QUESTION: How can autonomous archives become spaces of resistance in which physical objects, digital infrastructures and collective relationships continuously reshape one another? What forms of care, deceleration and shared governance can sustain them?

01 / OBJECT

Develop methods for collecting, hosting, storing, sharing and activating situated knowledge, as autonomous alternatives to dominant digital platforms.

02 / COUNTER-DATA

Digital records, material research objects, situated accounts and collective practices. Each context requires its own archival protocols.

03 / QUESTION

Who hosts, maintains and contributes? What happens when the archive can become an educational and physical device?

02 / CASE STUDIES & COLLABORATORS

Situated struggles / shared research.

ARCHIVE OF THE UNTAMED + EXIT / PARTICIPATORY RESEARCH — ALMA FUTURA / ARTIST COLLABORATORS

01 / ARCHIVE OF THE UNTAMED

Urban commons / collective memory / resistance

A participatory archive developed through artistic and collective practices around spontaneous urban ecologies, contested public space and struggles for access. Mapping, gatherings and temporary interventions connect digital memory with situated action.

02 / ALMA FUTURA — ARTIST COLLABORATORS

Feminist self-education / bodies / technologies

ALMA Futura develops collective research and self-education on women, bodies and technologies, including research into vaginal fluids where institutional access to study is obstructed. Their proposed hyperarchive explores an independent space for research materials, shared learning and resistance through autonomous technological infrastructure.

03 / SHARED RESEARCH QUESTIONS

Autonomy / deceleration / collective care

Who controls the infrastructure of knowledge? How can collectives set their own research rhythms, maintain their materials and make them accessible on their own terms? How do archives move between digital interfaces, physical devices and collective action?

★ WHEN PRESERVATION IS NO LONGER POSSIBLE, HOW CAN WE RESIST THROUGH CARE AND KEEP KNOWLEDGE IN MOTION?

03 / DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Web / autonomy / decentralization.

METHODOLOGY 01 / WEB. Experiment with autonomous platforms, self-hosting and distributed nodes as infrastructures of resistance. Examine how ownership, access, maintenance, deceleration and collective governance shape what can be researched and shared.

★ ALLY OR CONTROL SYSTEM?

ALLIED TECHNOLOGIES = tools shaped with their users. DESIGN / MAINTAIN / REVISE / SHARE.

OBJECTIVE: decentralized platforms / independent publishing / shared and revisable governance.
HOST / OWNERSHIP

Where is the archive stored? Who can maintain it? Independence is a condition to negotiate, not a property guaranteed by software.

ACCESS / GOVERNANCE

Multiple contributors. Revisable metadata. Consent, attribution and withdrawal. Visibility must not become compulsory exposure.

PLATFORM DECENTRALIZATION

Test distributed publishing and interoperable archives. Decentralization is a technical and social question, not an automatic guarantee of autonomy.

04 / PHYSICAL OUTPUT

Archive / physical device.

METHODOLOGY 02 / PHYSICAL. Server sculptures, installative devices, parasitic architectures and guerrilla mapping are possible outputs, not predetermined deliverables. Physical and digital are porous, mutually transformative conditions of the same archive.

ARTIST COLLABORATION / EXIT / PARTICIPATORY ARCHIVE DEVELOPMENT

ARCHIVE OF THE UNTAMED / EXIT

EXIT — Olivia Teglia and Lucia Fontanelli — is an artistic collaboration engaging with site-specific research, collective experimentation and public space. Their work intersects with Archive of the Untamed through shared inquiry into urban transformations, situated ecologies and collective memory.

Our collaboration explores the participatory development of Archive of the Untamed: ways of gathering situated contributions, building relationships with local communities and activating the archive through collective artistic practices and public encounters. Rather than imposing a fixed archival structure, the methods and forms of participation remain open to the people and contexts involved.

ARTIST COLLABORATION / ALMA FUTURA / PROTOTYPE IN DEVELOPMENT

SHELL AS HOST / ALMA FUTURA

ALMA Futura approached me to develop a hyperarchive for their feminist collective research and self-education on bodies and technologies. Conceived as an autonomous infrastructure for knowledge and resistance, the proposed shell-shaped server sculpture could host the website, store research objects and become an educational device. Like a jellyfish head, its core could connect to modular hardware tentacles: screens, projectors, sensors and other interfaces. Funding is being sought.

04 / Parasitic architectures

FLEXIBLE / SITE-RESPONSIVE / COLLECTIVELY ASSEMBLED.

DEBOUNDING

Public/private is not a stable binary. Examine who sets thresholds, grants access, monitors use and authorises presence.

TRANSLATION

Documents become projections, temporary structures and encounters. Spatial translation changes the reading of the archive; it does not simply display it.

FLEXIBILITY

Site-responsive interventions that adapt to different spaces, uses and local relationships. Their forms and duration remain open to negotiation and change.

05 / RELATIONAL GENERATION

People / relational generation.

METHODOLOGY 03 / RELATIONAL. Collective self-education, workshops, shared archival practices and networks of contributors sustain spaces of resistance. Knowledge is produced, contested and cared for together, at tempos negotiated by participants.

RESEARCH → SHARE → REINTERPRET → ACTIVATE → CARE. Movements and collectives shape the archive through their practices, needs and decisions; the archive can in turn support their capacity to organise and transmit knowledge.

★ A platform connects records. People construct infrastructures.

RELATION

Nodes are not interchangeable users. They are people, situated practices, uneven capacities, dependencies and shared commitments.

ASSEMBLY

The interface can facilitate contact. Collective decisions, trust, conflict and maintenance happen through human negotiation.

RHIZOME / TEST

Multiple entrances and lateral links. No mandatory centre. How does a network remain open without erasing responsibility?
