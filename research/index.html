MICOL GELSI
PROJECTSCVPLAY WITH ME

Multimedia artist and researcher working across public and digital spaces.

Her practice brings found and reclaimed materials into dialogue with low-tech tools and digital technologies.

Through living archives, site-specific interventions and participatory methodologies, she explores collective memory, inclusion and access to digital culture.

More-than-human relations, ecological systems and liminal territories recur as fields of investigation.

She works with ASAP and BLIVET and is a member of Sound Studies Forum.

Research

Bodies & Affects

Embodied experience, affect, intimacy, collective presence and the body as a site of relation and knowledge.

Digital Access & Inclusion

Access to digital tools, infrastructures and participation, with attention to openness, inclusion and shared technological agency.

Generative Archives

Archives understood as living systems that transform through use, participation and recombination.

Hydro-feminism

Ecological and feminist perspectives on bodies, environments, care, extraction and interdependence.

Live data manipulation

real-time systems, responsive environments and performative interfaces.

More-than-human Relations

Relations among human, animal, vegetal, territorial and technological agents beyond a strictly anthropocentric frame.

Surveillance & Politics of Visibility

Visibility, invisibility, observation, exposure, control and the politics of being seen or withdrawing from view.

Urban Wilderness

Research on urban wildness, contested landscapes, more-than-human presence and informal ecologies.
