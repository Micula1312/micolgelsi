A multidisciplinary performative project by ASAP, presented through performances, parties, workshops and installations between 2023 and 2025.

ABUNDANCE is conceived as a tropical party and a dancing ritual celebrating bodies liberated from a mentality of scarcity. An ironic journey through a techno-materialist and new-age universe towards a new era.
