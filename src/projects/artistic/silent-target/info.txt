EXCERPT:
Silent Target is an interactive installation investigating surveillance, visibility and the possibility of disappearing within systems of constant observation.

DESCRIPTION:
Silent Target investigates the contemporary imperative of constant visibility and the possibility of resisting systems of generalized surveillance.

The project draws on Pierre Zaoui's reflections on discretion as a political practice and form of resistance. Rather than proposing complete disappearance, it considers invisibility as something selective: the possibility of temporarily withdrawing from the continuous demand to be visible.

The installation reverses the usual architecture of surveillance. Hidden cameras and a visible control room expose the mechanisms through which bodies are observed, captured and classified. Surveillance itself becomes visible and assumes a sculptural presence.

The project questions the right to hide: when the apparatus of observation is placed openly in front of us, is it still possible to escape its gaze?
