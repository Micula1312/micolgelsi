MICOL GELSI
PROJECTSCVPLAY WITH ME

Multimedia artist and researcher working across public and digital spaces.

Her practice brings found and reclaimed materials into dialogue with low-tech tools and digital technologies.

Through living archives, site-specific interventions and participatory methodologies, she explores collective memory, inclusion and access to digital culture.

More-than-human relations, ecological systems and liminal territories recur as fields of investigation.

She works with ASAP and BLIVET and is a member of Sound Studies Forum.
