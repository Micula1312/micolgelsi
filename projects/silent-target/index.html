Silent Target

Silent Target is an interactive installation investigating visibility, privacy and the possibility of withdrawing from systems of constant observation.

Silent Target investigates the contemporary imperative of constant visibility and the possibility of resisting systems of generalized observation.

The project draws on Pierre Zaoui’s reflections on discretion as a political practice and form of resistance. Rather than proposing complete disappearance, it considers invisibility as something selective: the possibility of temporarily withdrawing from the continuous demand to be visible.

The installation reverses the usual architecture of observation. Cameras and a control room expose the mechanisms through which bodies are observed and captured. The apparatus itself becomes visible and assumes a sculptural presence.

The project questions the right to hide: when the apparatus of observation is placed openly in front of us, is it still possible to escape its gaze?
